TOC Presents: An Evening with Blue Moon Marquee

Doors 6:30pm | All Ages | Bar w/ ID | Tickets $32 adv / $37 DOS 

*pricing includes all ticketing fees*

Blue Moon Marquee trio is coming to TOC Portland, featuring Jasmine Colette on a four piece floor drum kit while playing upright bass and singing, with A.W. Cardinal on guitar and smokey vocals and Howlin’ Pete Cormier on keys. Their original music moves through blues, jazz, swing, country, folk, jump jive, and Indigenous soul. Drawing on the traditions of artists from Howlin’ Wolf and Memphis Minnie to Django Reinhardt and Louis Prima, they weave these influences with Indigenous storytelling, poetic lyricism, and their own contemporary perspective. Their album Scream, Holler & Howl earned the 2024 JUNO Award for Blues Album of the Year, followed by another nomination in the category in 2025. Now touring with a full band, Blue Moon Marquee brings those wide-ranging roots together in music grounded in tradition while distinctly their own.

TOC & Abbey Arts Present: Leah Song (of Rising Appalachia)

Doors 6:30pm | All Ages | Bar w/ ID | Tickets $29-43

*Pricing includes all ticketing fees*

An intimate evening of song, story, and old-world sound with Leah Song of Rising Appalachia and Duncan Wickel.

Featuring songs from their newest record, My Hope Lies Down in the Mississippi, alongside Appalachian traditionals, old ballads, banjo, strings, and songs gathered along the way.


TICKETS

TOC humbly acknowledges that the Portland metropolitan area rests on the traditional village sites of the Multnomah, Wasco, Cowlitz, Kathlamet, Clackamas, Bands of Chinook, Tualatin Kalapuya, Molalla, and many other Tribes who made their homes along the Columbia (Wimahl) and Willamette (Whilamut) rivers and continue to live in this region today. We honor their enduring relationship to this land and are grateful to live, work, and present in this community.
